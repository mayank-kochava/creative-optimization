Kochava The Competitive Moat 8 / 11
Defensible advantages
Two moats competitors cannot replicate
01
Attribution-Trained Model
Every competitor scores creatives on visual features only — what they look like. Kochava trains on what actually happened: installs, ROAS, LTV. A model trained on 100M attribution events understands why ads convert, not just what they contain.
Why competitors can't copy this
AppsFlyer and Singular are attribution platforms first — they own this data too. But they haven't built the fine-tuning loop, and they're not going to open-source their model. The race is on. First to 5K training pairs wins.
02
On-Premise Deployment
Zero competitors offer true on-premise. AppsFlyer: SaaS only. Singular: private cloud add-on, not on-prem. VidMob, CreativeX, Segwise: all SaaS. Enterprise customers in gaming, fintech, and health verticals have strict data sovereignty requirements that SaaS cannot satisfy.
Why this matters for Kochava
Kochava already has the on-premise story for attribution. Creative intelligence on-prem extends the same trust relationship. No creative data leaves the customer's environment. Enterprise-grade, compliance-first.
What's no longer a moat
NL explanation (F2) was unique in 2023. In 2025, VidMob's Maddie, Motion's Insights Hub, and Superads Copilot all launched NL explanation features. It's now table stakes, not differentiation. Our moat is F1 + F3 + attribution training + on-premise.
Bounding boxes — still unique
AppsFlyer's "AI Investigate" shows a timeline of feature appearances in video. That is not the same as drawing spatial bounding boxes on the actual creative asset. No competitor has F3 as we define it — YOLOv11 + Grounding DINO spatial overlay.
