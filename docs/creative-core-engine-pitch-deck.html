Why Kochava's attribution data is the unlock
Most creative tools score on visual features — what the ad looks like. Kochava can train on what actually happened: installs, ROAS, LTV. A model trained on attribution events understands why ads convert, not just what they contain. This is a product only Kochava can build.
Fine-tune economics
5K labeled pairs from Kochava attribution data is the target training set. Fine-tune cost per run is minimal. Result: significant cost reduction plus an attribution-aware model purpose-built for Kochava customer creative data.
